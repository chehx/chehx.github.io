Background and education

Previously, I was a Principal Researcher at AI Lab, Huawei Hong Kong Research Center, where I led research on visual generation and world models. I coordinated a 20+ member research and engineering effort spanning frontier model research, large-scale systems, data pipelines, evaluation, and product deployment.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from HKUST, and my B.Eng. degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University, where I graduated with honors.
